More and more men consciously take care of their health

June 27, 2026

I am glad that more and more men come to my practice with the conscious decision to take care of their health. Men from very different walks of life come to see me — some have families, others live alone — and that is exactly what makes it beautiful.

The tough-guy stereotype is slowly crumbling

Less and less often do I encounter the old stereotype of the tough guy who endures everything and never sees a health professional, because a man should not complain. That image is slowly crumbling, and I am genuinely glad about it.

The earlier you react, the more can be reversed

Because the truth is: the earlier we react, the more we are able to reverse. At the stage of functional changes, before tissues transform permanently, the possibilities of therapy are truly great.

Whether you struggle with muscle pain, digestive problems, stress that is hard to manage, or you simply lack energy — very often it can be clearly improved. You do not have to wait until something seriously hurts. It is worth taking care of yourself earlier, before the problem takes root.

If something has been troubling you for a long time, you are welcome at my practice in Opole. No judgment and no pressure. We will simply see what can be done.

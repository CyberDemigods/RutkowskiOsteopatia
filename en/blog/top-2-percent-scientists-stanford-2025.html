Science

Third year in a row in the world’s top 2% of scientists

October 9, 2026

A small reason to celebrate! For the third year in a row, my name has been included in the prestigious list of the top 2% highest-ranked scientists in the world (Stanford/Elsevier). In the latest ranking, covering scientific output for 2025, my position corresponds to roughly the top 1.16% of researchers.

Science beyond the treatment room

Although we usually meet in the treatment room, science also takes up a large part of my professional life. For years my research has focused mainly on implementing virtual reality in rehabilitation.

I am glad that the results of this research are noticed and cited by scientists from different parts of the world, becoming a small part of the international development of knowledge in this field.

Clinical experience and science

I have always tried to combine clinical experience with scientific work, because these two worlds should complement each other and, ultimately, serve what matters most — more effective help for patients.

If you are looking for therapy where practice goes hand in hand with current knowledge, you are welcome at my practice in Opole.
